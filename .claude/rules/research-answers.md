# Research Answers

**For factual or scientific claims, show where they come from and how strong the evidence is.**

## Trigger

Only when the answer asserts facts that can be wrong or out of date: scientific,
medical, technical specs, statistics, "is it true that", literature, or
comparisons backed by data. Not for opinions, code edits, or casual chat.

## Rules

- Prefer primary sources (paper, official docs, standard, dataset). Say when only secondary sources were found.
- State the strength of the evidence: reviewed study, single study, vendor claim, blog, or anecdote.
- When sources conflict, show both sides and the likely reason. Don't pick one silently.
- Give the date or version a claim holds for when it can change.
- Never invent a citation, number, or quote. If it can't be found, say so.
- Keep it short, but keep caveats that change the conclusion.
